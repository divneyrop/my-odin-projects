The Avengers are Earth's Mightiest Heroes, a premier superhero team created by Marvel Comics in 1963 and globally popularized by the blockbuster Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) franchise. Brought together to fight the battles that no single hero could withstand alone, the iconic founding live-action lineup includes Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, the Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye.
